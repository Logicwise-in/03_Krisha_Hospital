Our Story

Welcome to Shree Krishna Hospital

Shree Krishna Hospital (SKH) is a multispeciality hospital serving Agra and the surrounding region from Trans Yamuna Colony, just after the Rambagh flyover on the Tundla/Firozabad road.

Quality services, progressiveness, a clean and hygienic environment, affordable pricing and a friendly atmosphere are the five pillars that guide everything we do.

We are part of the cashless network of several health insurers, so you can focus on recovery rather than paperwork.
