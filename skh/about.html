About Shree Krishna Hospital

A multispeciality hospital serving Agra with quality care, a clean environment and fair prices.

Our story

Shree Krishna Hospital opened in Agra’s Rambagh area to give families nearby a trusted place for treatment, without travelling far. It is a multispeciality hospital with inpatient beds, a blood bank, X-ray and an in-house pharmacy.

Our team of doctors, nurses and support staff works to one standard: treat each patient the way we would want our own family treated.
